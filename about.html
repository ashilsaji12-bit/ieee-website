About IEEE CS MBITS

Who We Are

IEEE CS MBITS is the official IEEE Computer Society student branch at Mar Baselios Institute of Technology and Science, Kerala — a hub for passionate engineers shaping tomorrow's technology.

Our Mission

To inspire and empower students with technical knowledge, professional skills, and a collaborative spirit that drives meaningful innovation in computing and technology.

Our Vision

To be the most impactful student technology chapter in Kerala — a launchpad for future tech leaders, researchers, and entrepreneurs across all engineering disciplines.

What You Gain

  • Hands-on workshops & hackathons
  • Industry networking & mentorship
  • IEEE digital library access
  • Leadership & project opportunities
  • National & international competitions

Our Community

A diverse family of 120+ active members across disciplines — united by curiosity, driven by ambition, and connected through technology. Students, faculty, and alumni all belong here.

Our Story

Founded on Passion

IEEE CS MBITS was founded in 2021 by a small group of passionate students who believed that technology could change the world — if only young engineers had the right platform to learn, collaborate, and build.

Today, we are a thriving chapter of 120+ members, recognized by the IEEE Kerala Section and proud to be one of the most active student branches in the state.

IEEE Certified

Officially chartered IEEE Computer Society Student Branch, recognized by IEEE Kerala Section.

Award-Winning

Best Student Branch, Kerala Section 2023. 15+ awards across regional and national events.

Globally Connected

Members who participate in IEEEXtreme, ICSE, and other global competitions and conferences.

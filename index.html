Frequently Asked Questions

As an IEEE CS MBITS member, you gain hands-on engineering experience through workshops, hackathons, and real-world project builds. You receive international IEEE certifications, access to the world-renowned IEEE Xplore digital library, invitations to exclusive leadership summits, and networking opportunities with tech leaders and alumni worldwide.

All undergraduate and postgraduate engineering students from Mar Baselios Institute of Technology and Science (MBITS) across any department (CSE, ECE, EEE, ME, Civil) are warmly eligible to join. No prior coding experience is required — only genuine curiosity and drive!

Members receive official IEEE membership credentials, access to IEEE CS webinars, Computer Magazine subscriptions, discounted registration for international conferences, verified event completion certificates, and eligibility for IEEE student awards and travel grants.

You can volunteer in technical, design, content, and event management sub-teams. Check our Events Page for upcoming registrations or submit a membership inquiry directly via our Contact Page.
